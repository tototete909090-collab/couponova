The correct way to use it

  1. Copy the code

    Press the copy-code button or copy it manually.

  2. Search inside the app

    Open the SHEIN app, paste the code into the search bar and run the search.

  3. Review the coupons

    If the campaign appears, read the coupons available to your account: eligibility, cap and validity.

  4. Verify before paying

    Use the eligible coupon according to its instructions, and confirm its discount amount appears in the order summary.

New user questions

Are the coupons for all users?

The two coupons whose terms we could read state "new users only" and "selected products". We do not infer your account's eligibility from that.

Does receiving the coupons mean the discount is applied?

No. A "received successfully" message means coupons were received in the test, and does not prove they were used at checkout or that a purchase was completed.

Can both coupons be combined in one order?

The video did not prove combining is possible. Do not assume it is allowed; follow SHEIN's terms and the selected coupon. Do not add the two percentages together.
