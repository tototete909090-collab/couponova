What to check in your own account

  1. Search in the app

    S4T32 is a search code, not a guaranteed checkout coupon.

  2. Read the coupon terms

    Check eligible products, minimum spend, discount cap and expiry on each coupon.

  3. Verify the order summary before payment

    Rely on the actual discount shown for your own basket, not a headline percentage.
