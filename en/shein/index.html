Searched the code and nothing appeared?

The usual reasons a SHEIN search code shows nothing:

  • Your account is not new. The coupons observed in the documented tests were new-user offers, and the campaign may simply not appear for existing accounts.
  • Wrong entry point. The code goes into the SHEIN app search bar, not the checkout coupon field.
  • Country or currency differences. Campaigns vary by market and can change without notice.
  • The offer changed. SHEIN can end or rotate a campaign at any time; the log above shows what we last saw.

What to try: type S4T32 exactly, with no spaces, inside the app rather than the browser, then check the coupons section of your account.

Questions about the code

Where do I enter S4T32?

Enter it in the SHEIN app search bar. That is the entry point shown in the video, not the checkout coupon field.

What guaranteed discount applies?

The recording does not establish a fixed discount. It showed separate conditional coupons and did not show their application to a cart.

Are the observed coupons for everyone?

The two readable coupons stated new users only and selected products. Your own account eligibility is not verified.

Does it work in Saudi Arabia and worldwide?

The owner reports Saudi Arabia as the test country, and Saudi riyal amounts were visible. The country setting and a coupon applied to a cart were not shown. Availability for every Saudi account or another country is not guaranteed.

Does receipt of coupons prove a checkout discount?

No. The receipt message establishes that coupons were received in that recording, not that they were redeemed or a purchase completed.

Is Code Wafra officially endorsed by SHEIN?

Code Wafra is an independent website, not the official SHEIN website, and does not claim official endorsement.

What is the difference between a SHEIN coupon code and a search code?

A traditional coupon code is pasted in the coupon field at checkout. A search code like S4T32 is typed into the SHEIN app search bar; when a campaign appears, you collect its coupons and use them under their own terms.

Is there a SHEIN promo code for first orders in 2026?

The coupons observed in the documented tests were for new users, which is the closest category to a first-order code. Their terms appear inside the app after you search the code, and they may not appear for existing accounts.

Does a SHEIN discount code work in every country?

Nothing establishes that any code works for every account or country. Availability and terms differ by country, account and campaign timing, so rely only on what appears in your own app.
