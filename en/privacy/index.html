Privacy notice

Visit measurement with Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, such as pages visited, visit duration, device type, and approximate country. The service works through cookies and processes data under Google's privacy policy.

We do not send this service names, phone numbers, or account details. Because the link you arrive through can carry additions after the question mark, we remove all of these additions before measuring because any value in them could carry personal data, and we send the external visit source as the general site name only (like google.com or instagram.com) without its link details; visit sources stay recognizable in general, without tracking individual campaign details.

Your choice comes first

Measurement tools load only after you choose "Accept" on the bar shown at the bottom of the page. Choosing "Reject" prevents them from loading, and every page stays available either way.
